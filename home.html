About

The Public Service Request Tracker is a web-based platform designed to help citizens access essential local services with ease. Users can find trusted service providers, book services, report civic issues, and track the progress of their requests through a single, user-friendly application. Our goal is to make public services more accessible, organized, and convenient for everyone.
